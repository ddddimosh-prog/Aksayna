Our story

Dawn and night, same clothes.

D&N is named for the two ends of a training day. The first session at dawn and the last one at night should happen in clothes that feel right at both.

Why it exists

Clothes that do the work and then get out of the way.

Most training clothes are loud. They carry big logos, neon panels and slogans that have nothing to do with the session. D&N started from the opposite idea: choose a few good fabrics, cut them well, and keep the branding to a small ring on the chest.

Every piece is built around how it is used. The tee is heavy so it does not cling. The shorts are cut with room for a deep squat. The straps are plain cotton because plain cotton works. Colors come from a short palette of greens, sand and ink, so everything in the range goes together.

The brand is fictional and the store is a demo, built as a design portfolio project. The aim was a shop that feels considered, loads fast and works on a phone as well as on a desktop.

Principles

Three rules we keep.

Heavy on fabric

Dense knits and woven stretch that hold up to a barbell, a rack and a washing machine.

Light on branding

One small ring mark. No slogans, no giant logos, nothing that dates the piece in a year.

Made to repeat

Six colors across twelve pieces, so a new item always works with what you already own.
